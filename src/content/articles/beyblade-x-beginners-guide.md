Every Beyblade X top is made of three parts. Learn them and every product listing starts to make sense.

## The Blade

The Blade is the metal-and-plastic disc on top. Its shape decides how your Bey hits: Attack blades have sharp edges, Defense blades are round and heavy, and Stamina blades spread their weight to the outside so they spin longer.

## The Ratchet

The Ratchet sits under the Blade. Its name is two numbers, like 3-60: the first is how many prongs it has, the second is its height.

## The Bit

The Bit is the tip that touches the stadium. It controls movement. Flat, aggressive Bits race around the stadium; sharp or ball-shaped Bits stay near the center.

## Where to start

Pick one starter set, learn how it behaves, then swap one part at a time. That is the fastest way to learn what each part really does.
