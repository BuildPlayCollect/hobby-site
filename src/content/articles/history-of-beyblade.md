Two tops. One stadium. A countdown, a rip, and about a minute of pure chaos.

Beyblade feels like a modern invention, but its roots go back centuries, to a toy that started life as a seashell full of sand. Grab your launcher. Here's how we got from there to a stadium near you.

## Before Beyblade: the beigoma

Long before anyone yelled "Let it rip!", people in Japan were battling with **beigoma**, small traditional tops about 3 cm wide. The earliest ones were made by filling spiral seashells called *bai* with sand and sealing them with wax. Later versions were made of lead, then cast metal. [(Wikipedia: Beigoma)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beigoma)

The game was simple: stretch a canvas sheet over a bucket, launch your top with a cord, and win by spinning longest or knocking your rival out. Beigoma were well known by the Edo period, and their popularity peaked in the first half of the 20th century. [(Wikipedia: Beigoma)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beigoma)

Sound familiar? Beyblades were inspired by them. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade)

## Two misses, then a hit

Beyblade wasn't Takara's first swing at battling tops. Its predecessor released "Suge Goma" in 1995 and "Battle Top" in 1997, and neither took off. [(Tokyo Weekender)](https://www.tokyoweekender.com/entertainment/beyblade-25th-anniversary/) Third time's the charm.

## Generation 1: the original Beyblade (1999 to 2004)

The first Beyblades launched in Japan in **1999**. [(TOMY press release, 2025)](https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/release/202503105477) That same year, Takao Aoki's *Bakuten Shoot Beyblade* manga began in *CoroCoro Comic* on August 10, 1999, running until 2004. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade manga)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade_(manga))

The anime followed on TV Tokyo in January 2001, and reached ABC Family in the US in 2002. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade manga)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade_(manga)) Suddenly every kid knew Tyson's Dragoon, Kai's Dranzer, Ray's Drigger and Max's Draciel. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade characters)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Beyblade_characters)

These tops were made mostly of plastic. Hasbro took them international in 2002, and by 2003 more than 5 million had sold in the US alone. In the UK, Beyblade was the most sought-after toy for Christmas 2003. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade)

## Generation 2: Metal Fight Beyblade (2008 onward)

Takara merged with Tomy in 2006, and two years later the new Takara Tomy released **Metal Fight Beyblade** on July 12, 2008. As the name promises, this generation added metal parts. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade)

Known in English as *Beyblade: Metal Fusion*, its anime ran across four seasons from 2009 to 2012: Metal Fusion, Metal Masters, Metal Fury and Shogun Steel. [(Wikipedia: Metal Fight Beyblade)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_Fight_Beyblade) In 2011, Beyblades were the top-selling battling toys in the US. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade)

## Generation 3: Beyblade Burst (2015 onward)

In 2015, **Beyblade Burst** gave tops a dramatic new trick: take enough hits and your Bey can *burst* apart mid-battle. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade) Fun fact: that idea came from a colleague in a different department at the company. [(Tokyo Weekender)](https://www.tokyoweekender.com/entertainment/beyblade-25th-anniversary/)

Hasbro started selling Burst in the US in January 2017. The anime was a marathon, running seven seasons from Burst in 2016 to QuadStrike, which ended in December 2023. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade Burst)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade_Burst)

## Generation 4: Beyblade X (2023 to today)

**Beyblade X** launched in Japan on July 15, 2023. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade X)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade_X) Its big idea is **Xtreme Dash**, which speeds tops up so they crash into each other more often, helped by the Xtreme Rail built into the stadium. Battles were also shortened to about a minute. [(Tokyo Weekender)](https://www.tokyoweekender.com/entertainment/beyblade-25th-anniversary/), [(Wikipedia: Beyblade)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade)

It worked. Beyblade X shipped more than 28 million units worldwide by February 2025. [(TOMY press release, 2025)](https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/release/202503105477) The anime started in October 2023, and its fourth season premiered on October 9, 2026. [(Wikipedia: Beyblade X)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyblade_X)

## Rare tops collectors chase

Every generation has its grails, and Beyblade X is no exception. Its rarest tops often carry a "00" code (BX-00, UX-00) and come from events or store exclusives. We keep a list of them in our [Beyblade X parts list](/beyblade/parts/).

Two examples from a 2026 roundup by resale marketplace SNKRDUNK: a black **Cobalt Dragoon 2-60** Metal Coat handed out at events (listed from ¥5,800), and a **Samurai Saber 5-60K** Metal Coat in Samurai Blue for Japan's national football team, sold only at select Japanese stores from May 2026 (listed from ¥12,280). [(SNKRDUNK)](https://snkrdunk.com/en/magazine/?p=514909)

The nostalgia runs deep, too. Rare Beyblade X releases include tops named after the original four bit-beasts: **Dranzer Spiral**, **Driger S**, **Draciel Shield** and **Dragoon Storm**. [(Our parts list, from the Beyblade X Database)](/beyblade/parts/) The 1999 kids are all grown up, and they're buying.

## From toy to sport

The way people play has changed as much as the tops. What started as backyard battles on a bucket is now a sport with world championships.

- **More grown-ups.** Adult fans grew during the second and third generations, which Takara Tomy credits to social media. The company's goal is for Beyblade to become a sport that people of every age and culture can enjoy. [(Tokyo Weekender)](https://www.tokyoweekender.com/entertainment/beyblade-25th-anniversary/)
- **World stages.** The Beyblade X World Championship 2025 was held at Red Tokyo Tower on October 11 and 12, 2025. [(TOMY press release, 2025)](https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/release/202503105477) In 2026, there's even a world championship on Roblox (May 15 to 25), and the Beyblade X GP Final heads to Bangkok on December 12 and 13. [(TOMY press release, 2026)](https://www.takaratomy.co.jp/english/product_release/pdf/p260321_en.pdf) That's a short flight from Manila.

## How big is Beyblade, really?

Over **560 million units** shipped in more than 80 countries and regions, as of September 2025. [(TOMY press release, 2026)](https://www.takaratomy.co.jp/english/product_release/pdf/p260321_en.pdf) If you lined them up... actually, please don't. Just battle them.

From a seashell full of sand to a rail-riding Bey, the idea never changed: two tops, one winner, and a crowd that can't look away. Whether you grew up with Dranzer or just bought your first Beyblade X starter, you're part of a story that's centuries old.
