A Filipino double act going international, the most-delayed game in recent memory finally arriving, and a card combo getting split up. Here's your Thursday.

## Beyblade

Two Philippine bladers are going to Thailand. [Aedan Sulit and Juan Miguel Dantes qualified through the Manila tournament](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/gpfinal/) for the Beyblade X GP Final 2026, happening December 12 to 13 at ICONSIAM in Bangkok. They'll face qualifiers from 19 countries and regions, including Japan, China, the US and Brazil. Laban, Pilipinas!

## Consoles

It's finally here. [Grand Theft Auto VI launches November 19](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/) with a rolling midnight unlock that starts in New Zealand and moves around the world. On Xbox, you can also stream it on [Xbox One and Series X|S through Xbox Cloud Gaming](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/17/how-to-get-ready-for-november-19/). If you see a lot of people calling in sick today, now you know why.

Shopping season has started too. The [Xbox Black Friday sale](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/16/xbox-black-friday-sale-2026/) runs from November 19 to December 2 with up to 80% off over 2,000 games. Those prices are for the US store, so check what your region gets.

And mark December 10: that's [The Game Awards 2026](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/congrats-to-the-game-awards-2026-nominees/), where *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* on Switch 2 is up for Game of the Year.

## Trading card games

One Piece players, check your decks. Starting November 27, a [new banned pair](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/news/restriction.html) means EB05-001 Jewelry Bonney can't share a deck with OP13-001 Monkey.D.Luffy or OP01-002 Trafalgar Law. Alt arts count too. Meanwhile, [OP-18 The Dominance of God](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/products/op18.html) hits shelves tomorrow, November 20.

Magic fans can already look ahead: the next set, [Nauctis: The Sunken Realm](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-nauctis-the-sunken-realm), releases February 5, 2027.

## Esports

Pokémon Champions is getting a [balance update on December 2](https://champions-news.pokemon-home.com/en/page/877.html). Rock Slide drops to 95% accuracy and its flinch chance falls from 30% to 20%, so spread-flinch teams just lost a little luck. Screech, Metal Sound, Frost Breath and Steel Wing all become 100% accurate, and Flash Fire's boost now stays through Mega Evolution.

The [Buenos Aires Special Championship](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-buenos-aires/) top 8 teams are now posted, and our [Pokémon Champions hub](/esports/pokemon-champions/) has them. Next stop is the Latin America International Championships in São Paulo, November 20 to 22.
