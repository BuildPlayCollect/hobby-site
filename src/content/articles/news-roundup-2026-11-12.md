One dynasty ended, one classic came back, and a lot of people are staring at a download bar. Here's your week.

## Esports

**League of Legends.** The semifinals did not hold back. Gen.G [swept Invictus Gaming 3-0](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/79482-gen-g-sweep-ig-to-become-first-team-to-advance-to-the-worlds-2026-grand-finals) on November 8. A day later, Bilibili Gaming [beat T1 3-1](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/79483-bilibili-gaming-knock-out-defending-champions-t1-to-advance-to-worlds-2026-grand-finals), ending T1's bid for a fourth straight Summoner's Cup. That sets up [BLG vs Gen.G in the final on November 14](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/blg-beat-t1-worlds-2026-final-geng/). Snacks ready.

**Teamfight Tactics.** TFT is moving out of League's house. Patch 26.22 started its [move to a standalone client on November 4](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-22-notes/). Swapping between the two games is one click, and your old TFT cosmetics still work in League.

**Pokémon Champions.** Chiu Chih-Hung won the [Taiwan Premier Ball League](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-season-calendar/) on November 7 and 8. Next big stop: the Latin America International Championships in São Paulo, November 20 to 22.

## Consoles

Hey, listen! [The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/its-time-experience-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-reborn-exclusively-for-nintendo-switch-2/) is out on Nintendo Switch 2. Nintendo says it was built from the ground up, with new visuals, voiced cutscenes and orchestral music, for $59.99. Nintendo also says family group members can [gift each other digital games](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/coming-soon-gift-digital-games-to-family-group-members/) on the eShop from November 16.

Meanwhile, [Grand Theft Auto VI pre-loads opened on PS5](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/) on November 10. On Xbox, the game also arrives through [Xbox Cloud Gaming on November 19](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/), launch day.

## Trading card games

If you've been eyeing [Pokémon TCG: Mega Evolution, Delta Reign](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63514), it landed on November 6. Time to find out who pulled the shiny stuff.

Over in One Piece, [The Dominance of God (OP-18)](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/products/boosters/op18.html) arrives November 20 at $4.99 a pack. It brings Water Seven and the Holy Knights of God, with Saint Gunko and Saint Shamrock making their card game debut, plus a new Monkey.D.Luffy Leader. Collectors, watch for a Super Alt Art Nico Robin and an alt art drawn by Yoshitaka Amano. The cards become tournament legal 7 days after release.

## Beyblade

Takara Tomy's free [Beyblade X Casual Battle Day](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/beyblade-x/news/news260321_3.html) tour keeps rolling through AEON Malls in Japan: Omuta on November 21, Toyokawa on November 28 and Musashimurayama on December 6. Win 3 battles in a row for a sticker. Spend 3,000 yen on Beyblade X that day for a lottery spin, with Wyvern Hover 2-80GN as first prize.

## Tamiya

The big one is coming. Tamiya's [Mini 4WD Championship 2026](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/event/m4_championship2026) runs December 6 at Tamiya's headquarters in Shizuoka, as one-on-one knockout races. Only racers certified as 2026 Champions can enter, and entries must arrive by November 23. Tamiya also revealed the [course for the Mini 4WD Station Champion Decider](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/event/mini4wd/m4stc2026).
