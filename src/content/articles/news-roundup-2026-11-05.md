Two teams booked their tickets to the semifinals, one set hit shelves, and a certain open-world game asked everyone to clear their storage. Busy week!

## Esports

**League of Legends.** The underdogs bit back. Invictus Gaming [beat Top Esports 3-1](https://rg.org/news/esports/invictus-gaming-upset-top-esports-worlds-2026-quarterfinal-allen-texas-wei-mvp-first-semifinal-since-2019) in the first Worlds quarterfinal, reaching the semis for the first time since they won it all in 2019. Jungler Wei was named MVP. Then Gen.G [swept LYON 3-0](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/79472-gen-g-sweep-lyon-out-of-worlds-2026-to-advance-to-the-semifinals), so it's IG vs Gen.G in the semifinals on November 7-8. Dplus KIA vs BLG and T1 vs Karmine Corp are still to come, and the final is November 14 at Barclays Center in Brooklyn.

**Pokémon Champions.** Victory Road posted the [full Gdańsk standings](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-gdansk/): 758 Masters players, with Javier Varela's sand team on top. We've added places 9 to 66 to our [Pokémon Champions hub](/esports/pokemon-champions/), so you can see which teams went deep. Ranked Battles [Season M-8 is live](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-season-calendar/) until December 2, and Kaito Arii was crowned Doubles champion of Season M-7.

## Trading card games

Pokémon fans, [Mega Evolution, Delta Reign arrives November 6](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63514). Its Build & Battle Boxes come with one of four foil promos: Masquerain, Magmortar, Enamorus or Kommo-o. Pick your favourite weirdo.

## Consoles

Clear your shelves (the digital ones). Grand Theft Auto VI [pre-downloads start November 10 on PS5](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/) for anyone who pre-ordered, ahead of the November 19 launch. PlayStation suggests freeing up at least 212GB. That's a lot of screenshots to delete.

On Xbox, Game Pass [adds Escape Academy 2 today](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/03/xbox-game-pass-november-2026-wave-1/), with Football Manager 2027 Console on November 10 and Thronefall on November 11. Nintendo, meanwhile, [declared November 1 "Pikmin Day"](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/nintendo-declares-november-1-pikmin-day-in-recognition-of-all-things-pikmin/). Celebrate responsibly.

## Beyblade

Okayama is getting a free place to rip. A [Beyblade X free battle area](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/beyblade-x/news/news261104.html) opens with GiGO FANDOM SQUARE in AEON Mall Okayama on November 20. It's free for anyone 6 and up, and you can rent a Bey if you forgot yours.

## Tamiya

Mini 4WD's best-dressed cars got their moment. Tamiya posted the winners of [round 3 of the Japan Cup 2026 Concours d'Elegance Online](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/JC2026condeleonline.html), split into Livery and Free categories. There's one round to go. Racers in Japan can also check the [November Stock Class race dates](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/event/mini4wdstation_stockclass/eventlist.html) at Mini 4WD Stations.

## Keyboards

Two new group buys. The [Seed Plan Linedge 65](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127251.0) is a gasket-mount 65% keyboard from $365, on Unikeyboards from November 3 to 25. [GMK MTNU Dasher](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127280.0) is a blue retro keycap set inspired by the Data General Dasher terminal and the show *Severance*, on pre-order from November 4 to December 6. It has no Southeast Asia vendor, so factor in shipping.
