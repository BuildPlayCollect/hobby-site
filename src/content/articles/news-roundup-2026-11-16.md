A new world champion, a 25th birthday, and a card set that boldly goes. Here's what happened since Thursday.

## Esports

**Bilibili Gaming are the 2026 world champions.** BLG lost the first game of the final to Gen.G, then [won the next three for a 3-1 title](https://www.esports.net/news/gaming/bilibili-gaming-beat-gen-g-3-1-to-win-worlds-2026-after-knocking-out-all-four-lck-teams/) at Barclays Center in Brooklyn. Knight was named finals MVP. The wild part: BLG beat Hanwha Life, Dplus KIA, T1 and Gen.G along the way, so every Korean team that came to Worlds went home through them. It's the LPL's first title since 2021.

In Pokémon Champions, Argentina's Ezequiel Bustamante [won the Buenos Aires Special Championship](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-buenos-aires/), beating fellow Argentinian Bruno Quiroga in the final with a Mega Raichu Y and Mega Baxcalibur team. Next up is the [Latin America International Championships](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-season-calendar/) in São Paulo, November 20 to 22.

## Consoles

Xbox turned 25 and brought presents. [25 original Xbox games are now playable on PC](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/) through backward compatibility, including *Knights of the Old Republic* and *Morrowind*. Halo's Firefight mode comes free to *Halo: Campaign Evolved* this winter, and *Wolfenstein 3* is officially in the works at MachineGames.

Nintendo shared its [US Black Friday deals](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/nintendo-announces-black-friday-offers-to-help-spread-joy-this-holiday-season/). The Switch 2 + *Mario Kart World* bundle drops to $499.99 from November 22 to December 5, and an eShop sale runs November 19 to December 2. Prices in the Philippines may differ, so check your local shop.

## Trading card games

[Magic: The Gathering | Star Trek](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-star-trek) landed on November 13. Collectors are hunting seven hand-signed cards, each printed in fewer than 250 copies, plus shock lands framed like the Enterprise's computer screens. Over in One Piece, [OP-18 The Dominance of God](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/products/op18.html) lands this Friday, November 20.

## Beyblade

The road to the [Beyblade X GP 2027 Japan Championship](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/beyblade-x/event/g1_pre2026_27.html) starts with X-TREME CUP G1 qualifiers, the first in Niigata on November 23. Six winners earn a spot in the final in Tokyo on February 21, 2027. Qualifiers are for Japan residents only.

Shopping for the holidays in Japan? From December 26, spend 3,000 yen or more on Beyblade X in one go and get a random [Special Ver. bit](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/beyblade-x/news/news261113.html): Gear Flat, Taper or Disc Ball. It's first come, first served.

## Keyboards

Group buy watch: [DCS Nights](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127328.0) opens November 18, while [SWG Retroguardia](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127321.0) and the extended [GMK CYL Purple](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127128.0) both close November 23. Wallets, brace yourselves.

## Tamiya

A reminder for certified Champions: entries for the [Mini 4WD Championship 2026](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/event/m4_championship2026) on December 6 close November 23.
