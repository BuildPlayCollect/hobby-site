Only four days since the last roundup, and the hobby world still managed to cram in a starship, two lottery drops and a 3-0 sweep. Let's go.

## Beyblade

Two new Japan releases landed on Takara Tomy's own shop, and both come by lottery. The [BX-54 Booster ジークスペリオン 0-70LP costs ¥1,600 and releases November 21](https://takaratomymall.jp/shop/g/g4904810080763/). Same day, the [CX-20 Customize Set C brings 4 blades, 3 ratchets and 6 bits for ¥6,600](https://takaratomymall.jp/shop/g/g4904810077596/). Lottery entries closed October 18, and winners hear back on October 26. Fingers crossed, bladers.

**Unconfirmed:** [ToyPeople reports](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=115351) that four new Beys were shown at BEYBLADE X GP 2026 TOKYO, led by a BahamutBlitz BK1-50I Metal Coat: Black made for the GP Final in Bangkok. We couldn't find an official page for it yet.

## Tamiya and Mini 4WD

The fastest racers on the planet now have a date. [MINI4WD WORLD CHALLENGE 2026 runs November 22 at Twin Messe Shizuoka](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/event/japan/event/m4worlds2026), during Tamiya Fair 2026. It's free, but only 2026 Champions class national representatives and overseas representatives can enter, and entries must arrive by November 6.

## Keyboards

Big spender alert: the [Code:002 TKL group buy runs October 28 to November 28](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127224.0) at $696.90, with shipping estimated for late Q1 2027. That's a lot of keyboard, so maybe start a piggy bank now.

## Trading card games

Magic fans, set phasers to preview. [Star Trek previews start October 20, the full card gallery lands October 30, and the set releases November 13](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/where-to-find-star-trek-previews). Prereleases are November 6, but Wizards warns that [some products may arrive late in Asia-Pacific](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/star-trek-product-delays-in-select-regions), including Collector Boosters and Bundles. Play Boosters and Prerelease Packs are on track.

Pokémon players get a busy week too: [Mega Evolution—Delta Reign prereleases start October 24](https://pokemon.gamespress.com/MEDIA-ALERT-New-Pokemon-Trading-Card-Game-Mega-EvolutionDelta-Reign-Ex), with the full release on November 6. One Piece fans can mark [OP-18, THE DOMINANCE OF GOD, for November 20 at $4.99 a pack](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/products/op18.html).

## Consoles

[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 arrives October 23](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/16/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-october-19-to-23/) on Xbox, and [on Switch 2 the same day](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/upcoming-games-october-2026/). Want something gentler? [Peak climbs onto PS5 October 22](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/15/peak-climbs-its-way-onto-ps5-october-22/) with crossplay for up to four friends. Racing fans, the [DualSense Gran Turismo Limited Edition opens pre-orders October 22 at $84.99](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/14/gran-turismo-7s-spec-iv-part-1-launches-today-and-a-first-look-at-dualsense-wireless-controller-gran-turismo-limited-edition/) and ships December 10.

## Esports

Worlds has its last main-stage team. [Karmine Corp beat MVK 3-0 in the Play-In final](https://dotesports.com/league-of-legends/news/karmine-corp-beat-mvk-worlds-2026-play-ins), after [coming back to beat Cloud9 3-2 on day one](https://www.strafe.com/news/read/worlds-2026-play-in-day-1-recap-kc-takes-down-cloud9-mvk-dominates-furia/). The Swiss Stage starts October 23 in Allen, Texas, where 16 teams fight for 8 knockout spots.

Pokémon VGC's 2027 calendar keeps growing: [Seattle and San Antonio Regionals were announced](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-season-calendar/), for February 6–7 and February 27–28. The Nice Regional wrapped up this weekend, and we'll add its results once the full standings are posted.

See you Thursday. Bring popcorn for the Swiss Stage.
