A Japanese player winning in Brazil, a fresh One Piece set on shelves, and the start of Beyblade's long road to Tokyo. Here's your Monday.

## Esports

The [Latin America International Championships](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-laic/) in São Paulo drew 652 Masters players, and Japan's **Masahiro Taoka** took the title. He beat Emilio Forbes of the USA in the final with a rain team built around Mega Gengar and Mega Swampert. Japan put three players in the top 5, and the full top 8 plus places 9 to 25 are now on our [Pokémon Champions hub](/esports/pokemon-champions/).

Closer to home, Sow Wei Harng won the [Malaysia Premier Ball League](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-malaysia-pbl/) in Kuala Lumpur with Mega Raichu Y and Mega Salamence. Next up are the [Stuttgart and Las Vegas Regionals](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-season-calendar/), and both Masters divisions are already sold out. Remember the [balance update on December 2](https://champions-news.pokemon-home.com/en/page/877.html), which trims Rock Slide's accuracy and flinch chance.

## Trading card games

[One Piece OP-18 The Dominance of God](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/products/op18.html) landed on November 20, bringing Water Seven and the Holy Knights of God to the table. Before you build around it, note the [new banned pair](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/news/restriction.html) that starts November 27: Jewelry Bonney (EB05-001) can't share a deck with Monkey.D.Luffy (OP13-001) or Trafalgar Law (OP01-002).

## Beyblade

Japan's [X-TREME CUP G1 2026-27](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/beyblade-x/event/g1_pre2026_27.html) qualifiers start today in Niigata. Six winners across the series earn spots at the Beyblade X GP 2027 Japan Championship in Tokyo on February 21, 2027. Meanwhile, Filipino bladers Aedan Sulit and Juan Miguel Dantes are counting down to the [GP Final in Bangkok](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/gpfinal/) on December 12 to 13.

## Keyboards

Fresh group buys to tempt your wallet: the [Ursus 65% keyboard](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127337.0) opens November 25, [GMK CYL Neon Vice](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127331.0) is on pre-order until December 15, and [Enso-E by Clever](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=126155.0) runs December 8 to 22. Today is your last chance for SWG Retroguardia and GMK CYL Purple, which close November 23.

## Consoles

A quieter week after the GTA VI launch. If you're in the US, Nintendo's [Switch 2 Holiday Experience](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/play-nintendo-switch-2-and-discover-gift-ideas-at-the-nintendo-switch-2-holiday-experience-starting-nov-25/) lets you try games and hunt for gift ideas from November 25.
