Buying single cards is usually cheaper than opening packs to chase one card. Here is where to look.

## Local game stores

You can inspect the card before paying and you support the scene that runs your tournaments.

## Online marketplaces

Biggest selection and easy price comparison. Check seller ratings, photos of the actual card, and the return policy.

## Trade nights

Great for trading duplicates. Agree on prices using a public price guide so both sides feel it was fair.
