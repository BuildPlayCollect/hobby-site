Nintendo began in Kyoto making hanafuda playing cards, long before video games existed.

## The idea that keeps coming back

Rather than chase the most powerful hardware, Nintendo tends to bet on a new way to play: a handheld, motion controls, a console you can take out of the house.

## Why collectors care

Its consoles, limited editions and game boxes are some of the most collected items in gaming.
