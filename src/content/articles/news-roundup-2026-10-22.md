Three days, one starship full of Borg, a few hundred very pretty Mini 4WD cars and the biggest board game fair on Earth. Pull up a chair.

## Tamiya and Mini 4WD

Speed isn't everything. Tamiya posted the [Round 2 winners of the Japan Cup 2026 Concours d'Elegance ONLINE](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/JC2026condeleonline.html), the contest for the best-looking cars, and Razorback builds picked up several of the awards. Want in? [It's mostly a photo contest now](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/m4condele2024/interview.html), so a good angle counts.

Back on the track, [Japan Cup Tokyo Round 3 runs October 24–25 at Shinagawa Seaside Forest](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/japancup.html). Entries for the November 7 Shizuoka EX must arrive by October 25. In Osaka, the [Tamiya showcase of fall and winter kits from the Hobby Show](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/newstopics/2026/0916showcase/index.html) wraps its tour on the same weekend at Joshin Super Kids Land.

## Keyboards

Two GMK sets for two moods. [GMK CYL Blanks](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127218.0) has no legends at all, runs October 16 to November 16, starts at $95 and ships late Q1 2027. Fancy a lizard instead? [GMK Salamander](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127233.0) runs until November 9 with a $135 base kit. Both have Southeast Asian vendors: Singapore for each, plus Thailand for CYL Blanks.

## Trading card games

Resistance is futile. Wizards revealed the [Star Trek set's mechanics](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/star-trek-mechanics), including Assimilate, which turns a card or an opposing creature into your own Borg artifact creature. It also showed [four Commander decks led by Picard, Spock, Worf and the Borg Queen](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/star-trek-commander-decklists), out November 13. Digital captains get there first: [Star Trek lands on MTG Arena November 10](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/mtg-arena/star-trek-event-schedule).

Pokémon TCG Pocket's [Dual Fusion expansion arrives October 29](https://pokemon.gamespress.com/Dual-type-Cards-to-Debut-in-Pokemon-TCG-Pocket-with-new-Dual-Fusion-Ex) with the app's first dual-type cards, like a Psychic and Fighting Mega Mewtwo X ex. A First Partners special pack follows on October 31. Planning a trip? The Pokémon Company says a [permanent Pokémon store is scheduled to open in London by 2029](https://pokemon.gamespress.com/Media-Alert-New-Physical-Pokemon-Retail-Destination-Coming-to-London), its first outside Asia.

## Consoles

The HD-2D turn-based RPG [Final Fantasy Resonance is out today on PS5](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/20/how-final-fantasy-resonance-uses-visions-to-change-how-we-see-the-series/), and on [Switch 2 and Switch](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/upcoming-games-october-2026/) too. Its party members equip Visions, summon-like helpers from past Final Fantasy heroes. Block builders, note that Nintendo lists [Minecraft for Switch 2 on October 27](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/upcoming-games-october-2026/).

## Esports

The Worlds Swiss Stage starts October 23, and the [Round 1 draw](https://www.sheepesports.com/es/articles/worlds-2026-swiss-stage-round-1-draw/en) didn't hold back: Gen.G vs IG, HLE vs TES, G2 vs Dplus KIA, and T1 vs LØS. Play-In winners Karmine Corp get Anyone's Legend. [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27334/swiss-stage-round-1-matchups-set-two-lck-lpl-clashes-dk-faces-g2) counts two LCK vs LPL clashes in the first round alone.

Pokémon Champions players have a busy weekend. The [Puebla Regional runs October 24–25 with Masters sold out, and the Thailand Premier Ball League is October 24](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-season-calendar/). Sign-ups for the online Global Challenge II open October 23, for play from October 30 to November 2.

## Board games

The big one is here. [SPIEL Essen runs October 22–25 in Germany](https://www.spiel-essen.de/en/), and Saturday is already sold out. This year it fills [all eight halls for the first time, with 921 exhibitors and more than 1,500 new titles expected](https://www.spielwarenmesse.de/en/mag/toy-market-news/spiel-essen-2026-set-to-break-multiple-records/). That's a lot of rulebooks to read.

See you Monday. Bring a bigger game shelf.
