A sheep driving a race car, a bug jumping from video games to cardboard, and a starship in your Magic deck. Normal week, then.

## Esports

The Worlds knockout club is filling up. T1 [swept Movistar KOI 2-0](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/79435-t1-sweep-movistar-koi-to-secure-worlds-2026-knockout-stage-spot), and Invictus Gaming [beat LYON 2-1 to become the first LPL team through](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/79436-invictus-gaming-defeat-lyon-to-become-first-lpl-team-to-qualify-for-worlds-2026-knockout-stage), joining Dplus KIA and Gen.G. At the other end, [Anyone's Legend and Team Secret Whales went home 0-3](https://www.strafe.com/news/read/worlds-2026-swiss-stage-day-4-lpls-top-seed-exits-winless-as-los-survives/) without winning a single game. The last Swiss matches are on October 31, and the [quarterfinals start November 3](https://lolesports.com/).

Pokémon Champions fans get a new team sport. Victory Road [announced the next World Cup of Pokémon VGC](https://worldcupvgc.com/), an online team event for countries and regions starting December 2, with an interest list open now. The Gdańsk Regional [streams October 31 to November 1](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-gdansk/). Our [Champions hub](/esports/pokemon-champions/) now has every Puebla Regional team, too.

## Trading card games

Remember Mega Golisopod, the bug winning Champions tournaments? It's coming to cardboard. [Mega Evolution—Delta Reign arrives November 6](https://press.pokemon.com/en/MEDIA-ALERT-New-Pokemon-Trading-Card-Game-Mega-EvolutionDelta-Reign-Ex) with Mega Golisopod ex, Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex and the return of Mega Rayquaza ex.

Magic is going where no deck has gone before. The [Secret Lair: Set Phasers to Superdrop goes on sale November 9](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/secret-lair-set-phasers-to-superdrop), at $29.99 a drop ($39.99 foil). One drop is called KHAAAAAAAAN, which is the most Star Trek sentence ever written.

## Consoles

PlayStation Plus subscribers get [Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider IV–VI Remastered and Monster Train 2 from November 3 to 30](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/28/playstation-plus-monthly-games-for-november-dead-island-2-tomb-raider-iv-vi-remastered-monster-train-2/). See-through fans, the [Prism Collection of clear DualSense controllers and PS5 covers](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/28/first-look-inside-the-prism-collection-with-three-new-transparent-designs-for-ps5-accessories/) opens for pre-orders November 10, at $84.99 a controller.

On Switch 2, [Minecraft now has Vibrant Visuals](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/experience-vibrant-visuals-in-minecraft-for-nintendo-switch-2/) (fog, shadows, fancy lighting), and digital Switch owners can upgrade to the Switch 2 Standard Edition for free. In the US, [T-Mobile customers with T-Mix can pick Game Pass from November](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/26/xbox-game-pass-t-mobile/).

## Tamiya and Mini 4WD

Tamiya's yearly zodiac racer is here, and it's adorable. The [Year of the Sheep 2027 Mini 4WD](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/products/95721/index.html) puts a painted sheep driver inside a clear K4 Gambol body, on a red FM-A chassis. It's out in December for ¥1,650. Also, Japan Cup Tokyo Round 3's [Open Class B was cancelled because of strong winds](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/japancup.html). Even Mini 4WDs can't outrun the weather.

## Beyblade X

Detective Conan just walked into the stadium. [Kid Hunt K4-70TK Metal Coat: White](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/anime/newsList/newsA261026.html) is offered through the November issue of CoroCoro Comic for ¥2,600, shipping included. In Kumamoto, a [free Casual Battle Day runs November 28–29](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/beyblade-x/news/news261028.html) for bladers aged 6 and up.

## Keyboards

Two new group buys. [MW Datenshi](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127249.0), a dark gray, red and off-white keycap set inspired by a TUYU song, runs until November 16 at $80 for the base kit. The [Linedge 65](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127251.0), a gasket-mount 65% keyboard, runs November 3 to 25 from $365. Missed the R2 Fossil? Its [extras sale is October 30 at 11 a.m. EST](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=125570.0).

See you Monday, after the Swiss Stage finally lets go.
