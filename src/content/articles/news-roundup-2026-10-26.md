A bug and a mouse winning together, two teams that can't lose, and a board game fair with no tickets left. It was a weekend.

## Pokémon Champions

The odd couple of the season is at it again. Mega Raichu X and Mega Golisopod carried Panyawut Noijan to the [Thailand Premier Ball League title on October 24](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-thailand-pbl/), and the same pair won the online [VR October Challenge #4](https://victoryroad.pro/vr-oct26-4/) a day later.

Over in Mexico, Kyle Livinghouse of the USA won the [333-player Puebla Regional](https://standings.limitlessvgc.com/0043/player/0319), beating Peru's Renzo Navarro in the final. His team had [Mega Charizard Y and Mega Froslass](https://metavgc.com/teams/charizard-mega-y-froslass-mega-by-kyle-livinghouse-at-2027-puebla-pokemon-vgc-regional-championships), a fire-and-ice combo that sounds like a cocktail.

Want in from your couch? [Global Challenge II runs online October 30 to November 2](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-global-challenge-ii/). Manila players, circle January 16: the [Philippines Premier Ball League comes to Pasay](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-season-calendar/).

## Esports

Three rounds into the Worlds Swiss Stage, [Dplus KIA and Gen.G are 3-0 and the first teams into the knockouts](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/79416-dplus-kia-and-gen-g-advance-to-knockout-stage-in-worlds-2026-swiss-stage-day-3). DK got there by [beating T1 in Round 2](https://www.strafe.com/news/read/t1-falls-to-dplus-kia-as-lpl-bounces-back-at-worlds-2026/), so T1 sits at 1-1. The LPL had a rough start: its teams [went winless in Round 1](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/79407-worlds-2026-swiss-stage-opens-with-the-lpl-going-winless), then bounced back. The [knockout stage starts November 3, and the final is November 14](https://lolesports.com/).

## Trading card games

Typos happen, even to pirates. Bandai posted [errata for EB05-033](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/news/notice_eb05.html): the card printed as "O-Robi" should read "Nico Robin". The set itself, [Heroines Edition vol.2, lands October 30](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/), or [October 31 in Asia](https://asia-en.onepiece-cardgame.com/). Bandai also updated its [Asia Floor Rules](https://www.carddass.com/bcg/en/pdf/floor-rule-forasia.pdf) to Ver. 2.2.0 on October 23.

Next up is OP-18, out November 20 (November 21 in Asia). On the English site, its [4th Anniversary store event runs November 20 to December 17](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/events/store-tournament-op18.html), with sign-ups opening November 6 and a 4th Anniversary promo card for every player.

## Consoles

Two very different ways to work up a sweat on Switch 2. [Nintendo Switch Sports Resort is out at $49.99](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/grab-your-headband-and-get-ready-to-move-in-nintendo-switch-sports-resort/) with 12 sports, including Thumb Wrestling (finally, a sport we've trained for). And [Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 hit Switch 2 on October 23](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/call-of-duty-r-modern-warfare-r-4-is-out-now-for-nintendo-switch-2-stay-frosty/) for $69.99. On Xbox, [The Wolf Among Us Remastered and Mouthwashing arrive October 29](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/23/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-october-26-to-30/).

## Tamiya and Mini 4WD

A safety note for anyone who gets Tamiya event emails. Its outside mailing service, Blast Mail, [had a security breach](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/newstopics/news_1982). Tamiya says it hasn't confirmed that any of its customers' data leaked, but warns of fake emails pretending to be Tamiya, so don't click strange links. On the track, [Japan Cup's Shizuoka EX races November 7, with the Champion Decision Match on November 8](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/japancup.html).

## Keyboards and Beyblade

No new group buys this time, but [DSS Drank Round 2 is open until November 6](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127176.0) with a $130 40% kit. Bladers, the [Beyblade X GP Final runs December 12–13 at ICONSIAM in Bangkok](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/gpfinal/).

## Board games

No tickets left, and no regrets. [SPIEL Essen sold out](https://www.spiel-essen.de/en/?view=article&id=421&catid=9), with the last ticket gone by Sunday afternoon and 230,000 visitors through the doors. That's a lot of people learning rules at once.

See you Thursday. Bring snacks for the Swiss Stage.
