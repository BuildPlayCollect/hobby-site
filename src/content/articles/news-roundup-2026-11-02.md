Third place, third place... first place. This week, a Pokémon team finally got its trophy, and a spinning top went to space camp.

## Esports

**Pokémon Champions.** Javier Varela [won the Gdańsk Regional](https://metavgc.com/tournaments/2027-gdansk-regional) out of [more than 750 Masters players](https://metavgc.com/teams/with/2027-gdansk-regional), beating Jude Lee in the final. Michael Kelsch and Ko Tsukide finished third and fourth.

Varela ran Mega Tyranitar, Mega Staraptor, Gholdengo, Milotic, Sinistcha and Excadrill. That exact six finished third at both [Nice and Puebla](/esports/pokemon-champions/). Third time lucky.

**How the final went** (Echo watched it live):

- **Game 1, Lee.** Mega Floette set up Calm Mind and hid behind Protect while Kingambit's Sucker Punches took out Gholdengo and Excadrill.
- **Game 2, Varela.** Sinistcha's Rage Powder made Kingambit's Sucker Punches fail, and a boosted Gholdengo's Make It Rain knocked out Incineroar and Mega Floette. Excadrill's High Horsepower finished Kingambit.
- **Game 3, Varela.** Mega Staraptor's Close Combat knocked out Kingambit on turn 1, and Gholdengo's Make It Rain took down Mega Floette on turn 2. Two turns, one trophy.

Philippine players, mark your calendar: the [Philippines Premier Ball League](/esports/pokemon-champions/) in Pasay is on January 16, 2027.

**League of Legends.** Worlds has its final eight. Bilibili Gaming [beat Hanwha Life 2-0](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/79459-blg-avenge-msi-defeat-to-eliminate-hle-in-worlds-2026-swiss-stage) to avenge their MSI final loss, and Karmine Corp and LYON also made it through. The [best-of-five quarterfinals](https://rg.org/news/esports/worlds-2026-quarterfinals-set-lyon-gen-g-t1-karmine-corp-dk-blg-tes-ig) run in Allen, Texas: Top Esports vs Invictus Gaming (November 3), Gen.G vs LYON (November 4), Dplus KIA vs BLG (November 5) and T1 vs Karmine Corp (November 6).

## Beyblade

Beyblade X is going to space school. At [JAXA's Sagamihara campus open house on November 7](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/beyblade-x/news/news261030.html), anyone 6 and up can battle for free, and a JAXA engineer will talk about the BepiColombo mission to Mercury with a Beyblade developer. There's also a display of the engineers' own custom Beys. Rocket science, but make it spin.
