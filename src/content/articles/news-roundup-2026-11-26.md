Record-breaking sales, brand-new battle rules and eight more race tracks. Here's your Thursday.

## Consoles

The numbers are in, and they're silly. Sony says *Grand Theft Auto VI* is [the biggest game launch in PlayStation history](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/23/thank-you-for-making-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-biggest-launch-in-playstation-history/): more than 39 million players on PS5 logged 683 million hours between November 19 and 22. That's about 17 hours each in four days. Sleep is optional, apparently.

Prefer go-karts to getaway cars? [Mario Kart World Ver. 1.9.0](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/mario-kart-world-update-adds-eight-courses-and-more/) landed on November 24 with eight remixed courses, including Mario Bros. Circuit 2, DK Pass 2 and Shy Guy Bazaar 2. It also adds two new Knockout Tour routes and saves replays of your latest races.

## Esports

Pokémon Champions players, new rules are coming. [Regulation M-D](https://victoryroad.pro/champions-regulations/) runs from December 2, 2026 to March 3, 2027 and adds 24 Pokémon and 4 new Mega Evolutions to the current pool. The full list isn't out yet, so start guessing. It lands the same day as the [balance update](https://champions-news.pokemon-home.com/en/page/877.html) that trims Rock Slide.

Before that, the [Stuttgart Regional](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-stuttgart/) runs November 28 to 29 with an official stream, and the [Las Vegas Regional](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-season-calendar/) on December 5 to 6 sticks with the old M-C rules. Our [Pokémon Champions hub](/esports/pokemon-champions/) now lists M-D too.

## Trading card games

One Piece players, this is your reminder: the [new banned pair](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/news/restriction.html) starts tomorrow, November 27. Jewelry Bonney (EB05-001) can no longer share a deck with Monkey.D.Luffy (OP13-001) or Trafalgar Law (OP01-002).

## Tamiya

Fancy a Mini 4WD weekend getaway? Tamiya's [TSUMAGOI CUP 2026](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/tsumagoicup2026.html) runs December 12 to 13 at Tsumagoi Resort Saino Sato. It isn't a knockout race: you earn points for every run and swap them for Tamiya goods. One of the two tracks is the Japan Cup 2026 course, and it's indoors, so rain can't stop you.

## Keyboards

The [Ursus 65% group buy](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127337.0) opened on November 25 for anyone who wants a new 65% board.
