Six hobbies, two weeks, one very busy calendar. Here's what actually happened since October 1, with the boring bits trimmed off.

## Beyblade

The big date for the year is set: the [BEYBLADE X GP FINAL 2026 runs December 12–13 at ICONSIAM in Bangkok](https://beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp/gpfinal/). Only winners of the regional qualifiers get in. Any Beyblade X product released worldwide by the end of September 2026 is legal, and the stadium sits on the floor, so everyone shoots kneeling. Knees, start your training.

**Unconfirmed:** [ToyPeople reports](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=114405) that the BX-52 Starter LusterDragoon 6-60LC came out in Japan on October 10 for 2,300 yen. We couldn't load Takara Tomy's own pages to double-check.

## Tamiya and Mini 4WD

Japan Cup season is nearly done. [The Championship Final is November 8 at Tamiya HQ in Shizuoka](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/japancup.html), open to the Open, Junior and Champions class winners from each round. One stop didn't make it: Tokyo Round 2 in September was cancelled because of a typhoon.

Shop racers get their own showdown: the [Mini 4WD Station Champion Decision Match is November 21](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/event/mini4wd/m4stc2026). Entry is free, but you need a qualifying ticket from this year's Station Challenge, and entries must arrive by November 10.

## Keyboards

Feeling purple? The [GMK CYL Purple group buy runs October 10 to November 10, 2026](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127128.0), with a $130 base kit and an estimated delivery in Q1 2027. If you missed it before, [GMK Rainy Day is back for a third round](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=127188.0), with a $135 base kit.

## Trading card games

One Piece players, check your decks: [OP14-020 Dracule Mihawk is banned from October 12](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/news/restriction-261001.html), Alt-Arts included. A replacement P-163 Dracule Mihawk Leader will be handed out at future events. Up next is [EXTRA BOOSTER ONE PIECE HEROINES EDITION vol.2 (EB-05) on October 30, at $4.99 a pack](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/products/eb05.html).

Over in Magic, [Reality Fracture hit tables on October 2](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-reality-fracture), after prereleases from September 25 and an Arena launch on September 29. Its [update bulletin](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/reality-fracture-update-bulletin) adds a new keyword action, Empower Jace, and a new artifact type, Heartwood.

## Consoles

Sony added [a faster AI upscaling option, QSSR, for PS5 on October 1](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/01/ai-upscaling-is-coming-to-ps5/). Marvel's Wolverine and Ghost of Yōtei are the first games with it, and Sony says PSSR is still the top option on PS5 Pro. On the Nintendo side, [Nintendo Switch Sports Resort lands on Switch 2 on October 22](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/News/2026/October/Upcoming-Games-October-2026-3208377.html), with 12 motion-controlled sports on Wuhu Island. Stretch first.

## Esports

[League of Legends Worlds 2026 runs October 15 to November 14](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/msi-and-worlds-updates) with 19 teams. The Play-Ins start today at Riot Games Arena in Los Angeles, the Swiss and knockout stages move to Allen, Texas, and the Grand Final is at Barclays Center in Brooklyn. Pick'Ems are already open, so go make some bold predictions.

See you in the next roundup. Bring snacks.
