title: Swiss Stage
hobby: esports
summary: A tournament stage where teams keep playing until they reach a set number of wins (through) or losses (out).
aliases: [Swiss Stage, Swiss stage, Swiss format, Swiss-style]
added: 2026-10-19
---
In a Swiss Stage, nobody is knocked out after a single loss. At League of Legends Worlds 2026, 16 teams play up to five rounds: three wins send a team through to the knockouts, and three losses send it home. Most matches are best-of-one, and the ones that decide who advances or goes home are best-of-three. Dota 2's The International 2026 also opened with a Swiss-style group stage that cut 16 teams down to 8.
