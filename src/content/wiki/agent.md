In VALORANT, an Agent is the character you pick before a match. Every Agent has their own abilities, and Riot says no two play alike. Agents come in four roles: Duelists win fights, Initiators open them up, Controllers shape how the fight flows, and Sentinels slow things down and guard an area. The newest Agent is Hush, a telekinetic Duelist from Guangdong, China, arriving on 27 October 2026.
