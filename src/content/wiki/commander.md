Commander is a Magic format built for a table of friends, usually four. Your deck is 99 cards plus your commander, a legendary creature (or artifact) that leads the whole deck. You can run only one copy of each card except basic lands, every card has to match your commander's colors, and everyone starts at 40 life. Take 21 or more combat damage from the same commander and you're out, so that big dragon across the table is worth watching. Ready-made decks are an easy way in: the Star Trek set gets four of them on November 13.

Sources: [Wizards of the Coast: Commander format](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander), [Wizards of the Coast: Star Trek Commander decklists](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/star-trek-commander-decklists)
