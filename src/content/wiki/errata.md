Errata is the publisher's official "oops" for a card printed wrong. The card in your hand stays the same, but the rules now treat the corrected text as the real one, so that's what counts at a tournament. One Piece Card Game fans just got a fresh example: Bandai posted [errata for EB05-033 in Heroines Edition vol.2](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/news/notice_eb05.html), where the card's name was printed as "O-Robi" instead of "Nico Robin". The fix covers its alternate-art versions too, and Bandai says corrected cards won't be available in some regions, so remember the fix even if your copy still has the typo.

Sources: [Bandai: Apology for errata and revisions to card effect text in EB-05](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/news/notice_eb05.html)
