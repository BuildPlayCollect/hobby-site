---
title: Upscaling
hobby: consoles
summary: Rendering a game at a lower resolution, then sharpening it up to a higher one so it looks crisp but runs faster.
aliases: [upscaling, AI upscaling, upscaler]
added: 2026-10-15
---
Drawing every pixel of a 4K image is hard work for a console. Upscaling lets the game draw fewer pixels and then fill in the rest. AI upscalers, such as PlayStation's PSSR, use a trained model to guess the missing detail, which usually looks sharper than older methods.
