Mega Evolution is the big glow-up of Pokémon Champions VGC. A Pokémon holding its own Mega Stone, like Charizard with Charizardite Y, can change into a stronger Mega form during a battle. Only certain Pokémon are allowed to Mega Evolve, and every regulation set so far has allowed it. Each set also grows the list: [Regulation M-C added six new Mega Evolutions](https://victoryroad.pro/champions-regulations/), including Mega Golisopod. That bug is already paying rent. Mega Golisopod and Mega Raichu X won both the [Thailand Premier Ball League](https://victoryroad.pro/2027-thailand-pbl/) and the [Nice Regional](https://limitlessvgc.com/tournaments/446).
