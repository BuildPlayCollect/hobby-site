Concours d'Elegance, or "Condele" for short, is the Mini 4WD contest for builders who care more about looks than lap times. Judges reward the car's appearance and dress-up ideas: paint, liveries, scratch-built bodies, the works. At a live event the judges can walk around the car, but Tamiya now runs it mostly online, where you enter a photo of your finished car, so picking the best angle matters. The Japan Cup 2026 online contest has four rounds, each with a long list of themed awards.

Sources: [Tamiya: Getting started with Mini 4WD Condele](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/m4condele2024/interview.html), [Tamiya: Japan Cup 2026 Concours d'Elegance ONLINE](https://www.tamiya.com/japan/mini4wd/JC2026condeleonline.html)
