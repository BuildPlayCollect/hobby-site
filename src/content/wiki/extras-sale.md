Missed a group buy? Don't cry into your stock keycaps yet. Extras are the keyboards or keycaps left over once every group buy order is filled, and the maker sells them later in an extras sale. Extras are limited and tend to sell out fast, so they usually go on sale at a set time. For example, Carolina Mech's full-size [R2 Fossil and Southpaw Fossil keyboards have an extras sale on October 30, 2026 at 11 a.m. EST](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=125570.0) on carolinamech.com, and the organizer warned there would only be a limited number.

Sources: [Geekhack: [GB] R2 Fossil & Southpaw Fossil Full Size Keyboard](https://geekhack.org/index.php?topic=125570.0) (extras post October 28, 2026)
