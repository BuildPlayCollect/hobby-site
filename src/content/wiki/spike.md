The Spike is VALORANT's bomb. The attacking team tries to plant it on a site, and the defending team tries to stop the plant. Once it's planted, the defenders have 45 seconds to defuse it. A team can also win the round by knocking out everyone on the other team before the Spike is planted. First team to 13 rounds wins the match.
