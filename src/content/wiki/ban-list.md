title: Ban list
hobby: tcg
summary: The official list of cards you are not allowed to play, or may only play fewer copies of, in tournaments.
aliases: [ban list, banned list, banned and restricted list, Banned/Restricted list]
---
Card game publishers update their ban lists when a card proves too strong or makes games unfun. A **banned** card can't go in your deck at all. A **restricted** card is limited, usually to one copy. Each update says the date it takes effect, so check it before your next event.
