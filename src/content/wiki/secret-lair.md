Secret Lair is Magic: The Gathering's pop-up shop. Wizards of the Coast takes cards (often old favorites), gives them brand-new art or a themed name, and sells them in small bundles called drops on [MagicSecretLair.com](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/secret-lair-set-phasers-to-superdrop). A "Superdrop" is a batch of several drops that go on sale together. The next one is the Star Trek themed [Set Phasers to Superdrop, on sale November 9, 2026 at 9 a.m. PT](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/secret-lair-set-phasers-to-superdrop), at $29.99 per non-foil drop and $39.99 per foil drop. One drop turns Weatherlight into the *Lower Decks* ship S.S. Cerritos, so yes, your deck can have a starship now.

Sources: [Wizards of the Coast: Secret Lair: Set Phasers to Superdrop](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/secret-lair-set-phasers-to-superdrop) (October 27, 2026)
