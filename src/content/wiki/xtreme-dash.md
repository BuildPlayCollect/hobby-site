---
title: Xtreme Dash
hobby: beyblade
summary: The Beyblade X move where a top's Bit gear catches the stadium rail and shoots forward at high speed.
aliases: [X-Dash, Xtreme Line]
---
Beyblade X stadiums have a toothed rail along the edge. When a Bit's gear meets that rail, the top accelerates sharply along it. This burst is the Xtreme Dash, and it is the signature move of the Beyblade X generation.

Aggressive Bits reach the rail more often, which is why attack setups rely on it.
