A prerelease is a store event held shortly before a new card set comes out. You get a sealed kit of fresh packs, build a deck on the spot from whatever you open, and play against other people doing the same. Nobody has a head start, which makes it one of the friendliest ways to try a card game for the first time. Magic and Pokémon both run them for most new sets.
