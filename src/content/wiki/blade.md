The Blade is the disc that sits on top of a Beyblade X. It makes first contact in a battle, so its shape matters most.

## The three classic shapes
- **Attack** blades have sharp, angled edges that hit hard.
- **Defense** blades are round and heavy so hits slide off.
- **Stamina** blades put their weight on the outside so they keep spinning.

Under the Blade sits the Ratchet, and under that the Bit.
