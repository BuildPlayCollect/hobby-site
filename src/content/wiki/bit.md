The Bit is the bottom part of a Beyblade X, the only part that touches the stadium floor.

## Movement types
- **Flat** and rubber Bits move fast and aggressively.
- **Sharp** and ball-shaped Bits stay near the center and spin longer.

Many Bits also have a gear that catches the rail around the stadium for an **Xtreme Dash**.
